### KREBS SUGGESTIONS

Make a video demo of the application in case something goes wrong. Either a collection of small videos or a master video that walks through the application (hit pause, talk about things, hit play, etc)

### PRESENTATION FLOW TEMPLATE
15 MINUTE  BLOCK - 10 min for present, 5 for Q/A
DRESS PROFESSIONALLY!!!
DON'T TOUCH THE CAPSTONE DAY OF!!!

* Introduction 1 min - who I am
	* Hello my name is __ 
	* What is it?
	* Why did you create it?
	* Personal story!!!
* Demonstration 4-5 min - what you built NOT WHAT YOU WANTED TO MAKE - MOST IMPORTANT!!!
	* Intro project
	* Overview
	* Main achievements (called 'pillars')
	* Extra features
	* Keep it simple and clear, refine explanations
	* Beats are the main interesting parts that carry you from point to point
* Technologies 2 min - how you built it DO NOT DO THIS BEFORE DEMO (so they know what you talking about)
	* LANGUAGES, LIBRARIES, AND TOOLS
	* REVIEW YOUR SLIDE IF AI GENERATED
	* Don't just show a bunch of code, show small parts if its important and simple
	* Keep technology macro, not micro
	* End explanations on a positive note (if something didn't work, end it positively)
	* DON'T MAKE APOLOGIES FOR SOMETHING NOBODY ELSE SEES
* Retrospection 1-2 min 
	* Don't point out flaws unless they're obvious
	* How did the development go?
* Conclusion 1-2 min
* Q+A - 4 min
	* Did you use AI? In what way?
	* If you could go back and change anything, what would it be?
	* Etc

OVERALL TIPS:
* Connect with audience personally
* Smile and make eye contact
* Use humor for a relaxed atmosphere
* Pictures say more than words
* KEEP IT SIMPLE
	* know your audience, they may not be highly technical
* Don't put too much on each slide
* Reveal info through animations
	* Audience will be done before you get to the info
* Make sure each slide is readable
	* Squint test (squint to see if clear)
		* Hard to read fonts are bad
* Rehearse, rehearse, and REHEARSE AGAINNNNNN
* DON'T TOUCH CAPSTONE BEFORE GOING ON IT GIVES IT GREMLINS
