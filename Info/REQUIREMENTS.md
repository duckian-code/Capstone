TEMPLATE PARAGRAPHS:
## Capstone Proposal
Capstone should challenge your abilities, pushing you to explore new research and development territories. The project should align with your prospective career path and personal passions, offering a valuable addition to your portfolio. Its core objective is to exhibit your current proficiency while showcasing your adeptness in mastering new technologies through a creative and technically impressive project.3

#### Project Proposal
Please provide a summary of your proposed Capstone project from a development perspective. Explain what you are building, the primary systems and features involved, the technologies and tools you plan to use, and the major technical challenges you expect to address. Identify the primary technical challenge, research area, or area of innovation that makes this project worthy of Capstone status and explain how it extends beyond work completed in previous courses. Focus on the implementation of the project rather than its story, theme, or content. 

Your response should be between 150 and 250 words.


#### Motivation
Explain the inspiration and rationale behind your project selection, including your personal, professional, or technical interests in the project. Discuss what you hope to achieve through this experience and why the project represents an appropriate Capstone challenge. Consider how the project aligns with your career goals, expands your skills, or explores opportunities within existing technologies, products, or industries. 

Your response should be between 100 and 150 words.


#### Technologies and Research Requirements
Identify the technologies, tools, frameworks, hardware, software, or platforms you plan to use in your Capstone project. For each item, provide a brief explanation of its purpose and how it will contribute to the project.

Present your response as annotated bullet points. Clearly indicate which technologies are new to you and will require additional research, experimentation, or skill development.

Example:

- Unreal Engine 5 – Primary game engine used to develop gameplay systems, rendering, and user interfaces. I have prior experience with Unreal and do not anticipate significant research requirements. 
    
- OpenXR – Used to provide VR headset compatibility. This technology is new to me and will require research into VR interaction systems and device integration. I plan to learn through official documentation, tutorials, and prototype development.


#### Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) is the smallest version of your project that still demonstrates its primary goals and core technical challenges. If you were forced to stop development at the minimum acceptable point, what functionality would need to be completed for the project to still be considered a Capstone-level achievement?

List the required features, systems, and functionality that must be completed. Focus on what the project must do, not on optional enhancements or future ideas.

Examples:

- The game contains a complete gameplay loop with win and lose conditions. 
    
- The game can save and load player progress. 
    
- The game creates a unique dungeon layout each time a new game is started.


#### Milestones and Timeline
Provide a tentative timeline for your Capstone project. While project plans may evolve throughout development, this timeline should represent your current expectations and demonstrate that you have considered the major phases of production.

Include key milestones such as:

- Research, Planning and Documentation
    
- Prototyping
    
- Core Development
    
- MVP Complete
    
- Testing and Refinement
    
- Final Presentation and Submission
    

Present your timeline in a table and include the major phases of development along with the estimated amount of time required to complete each phase. While timelines may change as the project evolves, your timeline should reflect your current expectations for completing the Capstone.
